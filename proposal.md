# FlickSix — Project Proposal

**Tagline:** High-energy, swipe-to-hit arcade cricket.

---

## 1. Concept Summary
- **Game Style:** First-person floating bat arcade cricket (no character models).
- **Engine:** Godot Engine (optimized for low-resource hardware).
- **Visuals:** Cartoon / low-poly aesthetic, vibrant colors, comic-book juice.
- **Controls:** Intuitive swipe-to-bat gesture mechanics with timing-based shot power.

---

## 2. Core Pillars
- **Pick Up & Play:** Zero complex button combos. Swipe angle sets trajectory, timing determines quality of contact.
- **Pure Game Feel ("Juice"):** Crunchy bat-crack SFX, hit trails, micro slow-mo impacts, and boundary camera shakes.
- **Iterative Design:** Mechanics first (ball delivery + swipe hit), features and modes added dynamically.

---

## 3. Initial Development Focus
1. **Pitch Sandbox:** Crease, stumps, ball trajectory, and bounce.
2. **Floating Bat Controller:** Mouse/finger drag-and-swipe detection.
3. **Contact Physics:** Basic angle + velocity calculation for shots.
